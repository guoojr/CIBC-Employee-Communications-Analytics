## Project Overview

This project evaluates employee communications performance on Viva Engage from September to November 2026, using a fictional dataset.

The objective is to identify what is working, what needs improvement, and recommend a practical next step for the communications leadership team.

## Tools Used

- **Python (Pandas):** Data cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, and KPI calculations
- **Excel:** PivotTables and data visualization
- **PowerPoint:** Executive presentation

## Key Findings

1. **Announcements drive higher visibility.** Their average reach was 64.7%, compared with 27.0% for standard posts.
2. **Most posts have limited audience coverage.** 31 of 42 posts (74%) reached fewer than half their target audience.
3. **Distribution strategy is an opportunity for improvement.** Important communications may benefit from more deliberate use of announcements.

## Recommendation

Pilot a priority-based communication strategy, using announcements selectively for high-priority information and standard posts for routine or engagement-focused content.

## Limitations

The analysis identifies associations rather than causal effects. Differences in audience, content, sender, and measurement windows may affect the results.

The 50% reach threshold is an illustrative benchmark, not an established organizational KPI.
